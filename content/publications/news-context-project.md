A news story is rarely self-contained. Understanding one usually means knowing
something the article itself does not supply — what else has been written on the
subject, what the people named in it have said elsewhere, how the discussion around
it has developed.

The News Context Project built tools to assemble that surrounding material
automatically and present it alongside the story, for journalists and ordinary
readers alike. The accompanying open-source toolkit supported applications such as
locating relevant discussion threads, surfacing quotations from an article as they
circulated, and pulling remarks from stakeholders named in the piece.

Source for the project was released as `NUinfolab/context`.
