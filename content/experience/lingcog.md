The Linguistic Cognition Laboratory at the Illinois Institute of Technology, directed by {{ person(id="argamon") }}, worked at the intersection of machine learning and computational
linguistics — on the non-referential properties of text, meaning rhetorical
organization and style rather than subject matter, and on computational stylistics:
capturing how linguistic style varies between individuals and groups.

I worked there as a contract programmer on NLP development for
**[ADAMA](/projects/adama/)**, the lab's IARPA-funded
effort to identify and interpret metaphor automatically across four languages.
