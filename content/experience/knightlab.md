Northwestern University Knight Lab sits between the Medill School of Journalism and
the McCormick School of Engineering, building and testing technology for how news
gets reported, told, and read.

My work covers embedded electronics and radio on one side and software and language
systems on the other. I also design and maintain the lab's DevOps and support
systems, so most of what the lab ships goes out through infrastructure I run.

## My projects at the lab

- **[SensorGrid](/projects/sensorgrid/)** — deployable air quality sensing networks,
  the data platform above them, and the sensor journalism work around both
- **[Privacy Mirror](/projects/privacy-mirror/)** — showing people what their devices
  broadcast
- **[Exploring Software Defined Radio](/projects/software-defined-radio/)** — opening
  the spectrum for reporting
- **[Creative Co-author](/projects/creative-co-author/)** — a drafting tool for first
  drafts
- **[Visual Recipes](/projects/visual-recipes/)** — design research on instructions
- **[NATO in Chicago](/projects/nato-in-chicago/)** — coverage and conversation around
  the 2012 summit
- **[SearchMinutes](/projects/searchminutes/)** — public meeting transcription and
  search; rebuilt the pipeline on Whisper for Michigan Radio's handover to the
  Associated Press
- **[News Compass](/projects/newscompass/)** — tracing science reporting back to the
  papers it cites
- **[Overstory](/projects/overstory/)** — news arcs read against their history, their
  precedents and the frameworks that explain them
- **[City Hall Monitor](/projects/cityhallmonitor/)** — searchable index of Chicago
  City Council records
- **[Knight Lab Tools](/projects/knight-lab-tools/)** — deployment, provisioning,
  content extraction, hashtag recommendation, and curation services
