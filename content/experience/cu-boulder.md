Teaching assistant for Digital Advertising Analytics in the Master's in Business
Analytics program at the Leeds School of Business: grading and academic support for
graduate students on the analytics side of digital advertising.

The course covers measurement and media buying, the same ground
[Socialcontext](/experience/socialcontext/) works commercially: deciding what a
piece of content is about and what that means for the advertising placed next to
it.
