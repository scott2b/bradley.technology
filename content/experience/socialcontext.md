Socialcontext builds contextual classification for the open web. A customer
describes the context they want in their own words, pages are classified against
that description, and the reasoning is shown with each result.

As CTO I own the system architecture: distributed crawling and harvesting, a staged
classification pipeline that combines conventional preclassification with LLM-based
analysis, and the serving layer on top.

Founded with {{ person(id="vargo") }} and {{ person(id="hopp") }}, both faculty at the University of
Colorado Boulder.

See **[Socialcontext Open](/projects/socialcontext-open/)**.
