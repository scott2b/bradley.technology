I worked in Nortel's network management organization, initially based in Richmond,
British Columbia, and later in Richardson, Texas. The role combined systems
engineering, software deployment, customer support, and extensive international field
work implementing large-scale network management systems for telecommunications
carriers and private networks around the world.

Much of my time was spent at customer sites, typically on assignments lasting three
to eight weeks. Projects took me throughout North America, Europe, Mexico, Australia,
and the Middle East, working directly with customer engineering and operations teams
to install, integrate, test, and commission production network management systems.

## Network management systems

The systems were distributed Unix environments built primarily on HP 9000 servers and
workstations running HP-UX. The application stack included HP RTAP, Informix
relational databases, and Nortel network management software that eventually became
part of the Preside product family. The primary product I deployed was Preside Switch
View, used to provide centralized monitoring and management of telecommunications
switching networks.

A typical installation involved configuring multiple Unix systems, storage, and LAN
infrastructure; installing and administering Informix; installing RTAP and Nortel
application software; integrating the system with the customer's telecommunications
network; and taking the complete environment through system and customer acceptance
testing.

Because these were production telecommunications systems, the work routinely crossed
technical boundaries. Troubleshooting could involve HP-UX, TCP/IP networking, RAID
storage, Informix and SQL, RTAP, Nortel software, or the interfaces to the underlying
switching network. I also used Unix shell scripting and Perl for system
administration, integration, and automation.

The field role extended beyond installation. I provided onsite system administration
and troubleshooting, worked with customer and Nortel engineering teams during
integration, trained customer administrators and users, conducted acceptance testing,
and provided consultation during the transition into production operation.

## Selected international projects

**Avantel — Monterrey, Mexico.** Implemented network management systems for Avantel's
rapidly expanding Mexican telecommunications network. Avantel was building a national
fiber and switching infrastructure during this period, with Monterrey serving as one
of its major network centers.

**Jazztel — Madrid, Spain.** Worked on the network management environment for Jazztel
as it built a new national telecommunications network in Spain. The Madrid facility
included a major Nortel switching supernode and centralized Network Operations Center
responsible for monitoring the national network.

**PowerTel — Sydney, Australia.** Supported deployment of network management for
PowerTel's new Australian carrier network. PowerTel was deploying Nortel DMS-100
switching in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane along with a centralized Network
Operations Center in Sydney.

**Saudi Aramco — Dhahran, Saudi Arabia.** Implemented network management within Saudi
Aramco's extensive private telecommunications infrastructure. Aramco operated a
geographically distributed network supporting facilities throughout Saudi Arabia,
including substantial Nortel switching and transmission infrastructure.

**Intermedia Communications — Tampa/Brandon, Florida.** Worked with network
management systems supporting Intermedia's Florida telecommunications infrastructure,
which included Nortel DMS switching and SS7/STP systems.

**MCI WorldCom — Frankfurt, Germany.** Worked on network management systems
supporting MCI WorldCom's telecommunications infrastructure in Frankfurt. During this
period, Frankfurt was a significant MCI WorldCom network center, supporting the
company's German fiber, metropolitan, and customer-access networks. The Frankfurt
Network Operations Center used Nortel Preside as part of its network-management
environment.

## From Richmond to a global organization

I joined the organization in Richmond during a period of considerable change at
Nortel. The network management technology had originated with Prism Systems and
evolved through several generations and product identities before becoming part of
Nortel's broader Preside network management portfolio.

The organization became increasingly global during my time there. I moved from
British Columbia to Richardson, Texas, reporting to management in the United
Kingdom, and spent most of the time at customer sites.

I was responsible for the whole distributed production environment: Unix hardware
and operating systems, databases and networking, the network-management application
and its interfaces to the telecommunications infrastructure.
