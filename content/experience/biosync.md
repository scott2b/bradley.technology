The Biodiversity Synthesis Center was the Field Museum's part of the Encyclopedia of
Life, the international effort launched in 2007 to put information about every known
species online. The center ran synthesis meetings, gave scientists from different
institutions a place to collaborate, and turned scattered biodiversity records into
data, publications, and software, in work that ran from phylogenetics and
biogeography through data mining to bio-semantics.

I worked on the center's website, a Drupal 6 build that was already in place when I
arrived. I developed on it, maintained it, fixed what broke, and gave day-to-day
technical support to the scientific and administrative staff as the center's
programs grew: PHP, contributed modules, content types, and themes.

The group was small and mixed, with biological research, scientific programming,
education, illustration, and administration in the same room.
