I worked as part of Perkins & Will's in-house engineering team in the Chicago office,
supporting the electrical design of major institutional, healthcare, research, and
higher-education projects. At the time the firm maintained integrated architectural
and engineering capabilities, with electrical engineering performed internally on
projects alongside architectural, structural, mechanical, and plumbing design.

My work involved electrical building-systems design and production during the design
and construction-document phases, working within multidisciplinary project teams on
large and technically complex facilities.

## Projects

**Samsung Central Research Headquarters / Nexus Centre — Taejon, South Korea.**
Electrical engineering for a major Samsung research and development project. Perkins
& Will won an international competition for the 2.43-million-square-foot Nexus Centre
research complex and was actively designing its roughly 550,000-square-foot Central
Research Headquarters in 1995 — conceived as a signature laboratory and office
building within the larger R&D campus.

**UIC Ambulatory Care Facility — University of Illinois at Chicago.** Electrical
engineering for a major new ambulatory healthcare facility. Perkins & Will provided
architectural and engineering services beginning in 1995, continuing through redesign
and design development in 1996. The facility was subsequently completed as UIC's
Outpatient Care Center.

**Genesis Medical Center — Davenport, Iowa.** Electrical engineering associated with
the development and expansion of Genesis Medical Center, consolidating and expanding
healthcare facilities in Davenport with new and renovated clinical spaces as part of
a substantial medical-campus development.

**Katherine & William Mayer Residences — Tulane University, New Orleans.** Electrical
engineering for a new university residential complex, with Perkins & Will providing
architecture alongside in-house structural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing
engineering. The completed residence hall consists of two residential wings organized
around existing campus courtyards and connected by a multistory gallery.
