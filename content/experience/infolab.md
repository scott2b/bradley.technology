Northwestern University's Intelligent Information Laboratory built systems for
finding, contextualizing, and making sense of information — much of it aimed at
journalism and the problem of what a reader needs to know that a story does not say.

## Projects

- **[The News Context Project](/projects/news-context/)** — an open-source toolkit for assembling the context
  around a news article: related discussion, circulating quotations, and remarks from
  the stakeholders a story names
- **[The Local Angle](/projects/local-angle/)** — surfacing national stories with local significance by linking
  articles to the places behind the people and organizations in them
- **[Listening Post](/projects/listening-post/)** — letting organizations respond directly to questions and
  comments on social platforms
