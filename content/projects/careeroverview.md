CareerOverview.com was a career and college-information site generated from data.
Northwestern's [InfoLab](/experience/infolab/) built it, before large language
models: a large site produced from structured sources such as the Bureau of Labor
Statistics, covering the occupations, what they pay, what they require, and the
schools and degree programs that lead to them. It was an early step toward what
became Narrative Science.

{{ figure(src="/images/projects/careeroverview.jpg", alt="The CareerOverview.com home page, with its three-step degree match funnel") }}

The generated pages sat on crossing directories: nineteen career families, forty-odd
industries, every state, and degree programs by level, category, and subject. A
three-step degree finder at the top and bottom of the home page led to matching
programs.

I worked on it with the [InfoLab team](/experience/infolab/#people).
