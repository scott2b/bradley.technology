Listening Post followed user sentiment and engagement with brands and products in
channels like Twitter and YouTube, and harvested branding opportunities from them.
With listening intelligence developed at the lab, it surfaced the individual user
stories with clear potential for engagement and intent, to inform business
decisions.

{{ figure(src="/images/projects/listening-post-dashboard.webp", alt="The Listening Post dashboard for Abercrombie & Fitch: an Attitudes chart over three weeks of October 2009, filtered by emotion, topic, and status, with tabs for posts, users, filters, and respond", caption="The dashboard, October 2009.") }}

It was a working application, built in 2009 as a pilot engagement with the
marketing agency Performics. I worked on it with the
[InfoLab team](/experience/infolab/#people).
