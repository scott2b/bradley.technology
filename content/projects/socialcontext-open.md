[Open](https://open.socialcontext.ai) is Socialcontext's open-vocabulary approach to
contextual classification. In place of a fixed set of categories such as the IAB
Content Taxonomy, Open lets a user describe the context they care about in their own
terms and classifies against that description.

The platform behind it crawls and harvests open-web content at scale, then runs it
through a staged pipeline: conventional preclassification to narrow the field,
followed by LLM-based classification that labels each page and explains its
reasoning. Classification happens at the URL level, and the rationale is shown with
each result.
