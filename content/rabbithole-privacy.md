rabbithole is a personal command-line tool that Scott Bradley wrote and runs for himself. It is not offered to anyone else.

**What it reads.** With read-only permission to its owner's YouTube account, it reads the list of videos that account has liked, and the public details of those videos.

**What it does with it.** For each liked video, it downloads the video's public captions and sends them, with the video's details, to an AI model provider to write a summary note. The note is saved on the owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored on that computer too.

**What it does not do.** It reads no one else's data. It makes no changes to the YouTube account. It does not sell or share data, show ads, or collect analytics.

**Revoking access.** Permission can be withdrawn at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
